# Pattern: Eternal September (Onboarding-Rate Shock)

**Topic:** `eternal-september`  
**Pattern ID:** `P-002`

## Thesis
Communities with implicit apprenticeship collapse when the rate of new arrivals exceeds the culture’s teaching capacity.

## Failure mode / principle
Scaling access without scaling moderation, onboarding affordances, and norms transmission produces quality collapse and conflict amplification.

## Conditions
- Sudden/continuous influx of newcomers
- Norms learned socially rather than documented
- Weak or informal moderation structures

## Symptoms
- Repeated norm violations
- Veteran flight and rising hostility
- Increased noise, spam, and trolling

## Countermeasures
- Explicit onboarding docs (“netiquette”-style)
- Moderation capacity scaling and tooling
- Segmentation: beginner spaces vs expert spaces

## Modern analogs
- Rapidly growing subreddits / Discord servers
- Open-source projects after a viral spike
